Validation techniques:

i.) Requirements review by stakeholders
ii.) Prototype validation with users
iii.) Test-case generation: "app should be fast" is vague, so it
      becomes "load within 3sec".
iv.) Each requirement checked for being correct, complete,
     consistent & unambiguous.
v.) Traceability Matrix (RTM):
