Q-2.) Requirements Engineering:

Requirements Engineering is the process of finding,
documenting and checking what the system should
do.

Fig. 2: RE Process
[Diagram showing the RE Process flow]
Feasibility -> Elicitation -> Specification -> Validation
                <- Requirements Management <-

Stakeholders:                     Needs

Customers                         Simple UI, safe payment, live tracking


Marketing                         Recommendations, offers, notifications


Logistics                         Delivery Status, courier, API integration


Management                        Low-cost, on-time launch.

Elicitation Techniques:

i-) Interviews with marketing and logistics heads

ii-) Online survey for existing customers

iii-) JAD Workshop: all department together to settle conflicts,
e.g., marketing wants many notifications but customers
don't.
