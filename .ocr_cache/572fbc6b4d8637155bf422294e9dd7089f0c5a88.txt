ShopEase is a mid-sized e-commerce company
launching a mobile app with personalized recomm-
endations, a secure payment gateway and real-
time order tracking.
Constraints: limited resources & a strict 6-months
deadline

Q-1.) Choice of Process Model:

| Criteria | Waterfall | Spiral | Agile (Scrum) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Handles changing requirements | No (x) | Yes (v) | Yes (vv) |
| Fits 6-months deadline | No | No | Yes (vv) |
| Low Cost | Yes | No | Yes |
| Early working product | No | - | Yes |
| Risk handling | No | Yes | Yes |

Why not Waterfall?
It is linear and testing comes only at the end.
If payment integration fails in month 5, there is no
time to fix it. Marketing requirements will also keep
changing.
Why not Spiral?
It is best for risk, but each loop needs detailed risk
analysis by experts, which is costly and slow. Shopease
has limited resources.
