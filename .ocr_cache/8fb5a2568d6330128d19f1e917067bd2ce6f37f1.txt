c.) Team Co-ordination:

1.) Scrum ceremonies:
    daily stand-up (what did I do yesterday? what will I do
    today? Any blockers?), sprint planning, review and
    retrospective.

2.) Tools:
    Jira/Trello for stracking of task
    Slack/Teams for communication
    Git/Github for version control
    Confluence for documentation

3.) Cross-functional team:
    developers, testers and designers work together in the
    same sprint.

4.) Clear responsibility: a RACI chart (Responsible, Accountable,
    consulted, informed)

5.) Regular Stakeholders meetings with marketing and logistics
    teams every sprint review.

6.) Configuration management: branching strategy, code
    reviews and versioning to avoid conflicts
