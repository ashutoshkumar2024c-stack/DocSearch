Central Library FAQ

Opening hours

The library is open from 8 am to 10 pm on weekdays and from 9 am to 5 pm on Saturdays. It is closed on Sundays.

Borrowing rules

  • Students can borrow up to 4 books at a time for 14 days.
  • Faculty can borrow up to 10 books for 60 days.
  • A late fee of Rs 5 per day applies to overdue books.

Lost books

A lost book must be replaced or paid for at twice its listed price.
