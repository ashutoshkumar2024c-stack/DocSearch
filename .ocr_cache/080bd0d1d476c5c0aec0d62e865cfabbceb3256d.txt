Selected: Agile (Scrum) with spiral-style risk ana-
lysis.

Justification:

i.) Timeboxing : 6 months = 12 sprints of 2 weeks . The
deadline stays fixed and only scope is
adjusted.

ii.) Priority based : Important features (login, catalogue,
delivery payment, tracking) are built first, less
important ones can be postponed.

iii.) Accepts : New requirements go into the Product
change Backlog.

iv.) Continuous : Testing in every sprint catches integration
testing failures early.

v.) Better : Daily stand-ups remove communication
communication gaps.

vi.) Risk Review : Before each release for high-risk parts
(from spiral) like payment.
